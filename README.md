# Drift Third Person Test V3.8

Major chassis change:
- Front and rear axles now calculate separate slip angles.
- Front lateral force acts ahead of the center of gravity.
- Rear lateral force acts behind the center of gravity.
- Those axle forces create real yaw torque around the CG.
- Removed the large artificial steering/drift yaw helpers.
- Rear wheelspin only reduces rear tire capacity.
- Rear vertical load, suspension response, tire pressure, slip curve, and locked differential remain active.
- A donut should now be possible with steady throttle + steady steering if rear capacity is low enough.
- Added F/R HUD showing front/rear axle slip angles in degrees.
